# P09 — Vulnerability Exposure

Status: `SOURCE_COMPLETE / SCOPED_VALIDATION_PASS`

## Authority

- Route: `/devices/vulnerabilities`
- Archetype: `Exposure Analytics + Queue`
- Primary users: vulnerability manager, security architect
- Primary action: `Create remediation item`

## Core task

Turn scanner and telemetry observations into a prioritized business-exposure view. The page combines exploit evidence, match confidence, business criticality, due state and remediation ownership without presenting an ITSM ticket closure as vulnerability remediation completion.

## First viewport and main workspace

1. Search and risk/exploitability/service/remediation/due filters.
2. Five decision KPIs: critical exploitable assets, overdue exposures, unverified matches, mean age high risk and accepted-risk assets.
3. 90-day exposure-risk trend with exact-data fallback.
4. Risk-driver breakdown and business-service × due-state matrix.
5. Dense exposure remediation queue.
6. Exposure detail Flyout and risk-acceptance Modal.

## Difference contract

### P09 versus P08

1. P09 begins with exposure risk analytics and remediation pressure; P08 begins with canonical identity and source reconciliation.
2. P09 primary objects are exposure records joining vulnerability, asset, exploitability and due state; P08 primary objects are canonical assets and observations.
3. P09 actions create remediation/change, accept risk or mark false match; P08 actions reconcile observations and pivot to Asset 360/device status.
4. P09 detail explains exploit evidence and closure validation; P08 detail explains identity provenance and field ownership.

### P09 versus P10

1. P09 is portfolio analytics plus remediation prioritization; P10 is evidence-oriented match adjudication.
2. P09 foregrounds trend, risk drivers and service due-state; P10 foregrounds package/CPE evidence, match confidence and false-positive resolution.
3. P09 risk acceptance is a governed exception; P10 decisions confirm or reject a specific vulnerability-to-asset match.
